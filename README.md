Entry for the make.fun bounty [One Line of Earned Pride](https://make.fun/bounty/u-mang-one-line-of-earned-pride-a0138557).

A pure-stdlib Python program (`main.py`) writes one line of text to `/output/entry.txt` — a quiet, earned moment of pride for someone in a scene. The line:

> At seventy-two, after a year of physio, she climbed the forty-two steps to her flat without once touching the rail.

115 characters. The program runs offline and produces byte-identical output on every run.
